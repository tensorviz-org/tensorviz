### Share a graph

**Add to tensorViz**, in the Canvas's model menu, puts the graph on tensorviz.dev in
one click; the first time, you sign in with Google in your browser. The graph arrives
private, and the page that opens lets you choose who can see it, tensorViz members or
anyone with the link, and copy the link.

- Rename it, describe it and start a discussion about its layers on the website.
- **Open Canvas in Browser** still exports a versioned `.tensorviz.json` snapshot,
  which **Open graph file viewer** opens offline, without VS Code, Python or an
  account. Export refuses missing, stale-target or oversized evidence rather than
  saving an incomplete snapshot.
